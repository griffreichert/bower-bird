# Invariants

Principles that always hold, however the project is built. Mechanics, paths, and
lifecycle live in the project plan — not here. If a line names a file or a
folder, it's in the wrong document.

### Soul
- **Enhance, never replace.** It augments my learning; it never does it for me.
  What I read, highlighted, or wrote is never erased or summarized away. The
  curated distillation may grow; my own thinking is never clobbered.
- **Everything sent is shelved.** The share is the shelving signal — every send
  becomes a brain node immediately, no reading gate first. Antilibrary
  (Eco/Taleb): the shelf's value includes the unengaged. Recall — peck plus the
  LLM judge — is the learning event, and the only honest ledger of what's
  actually known; reading was never it.
- **Distill at ingest.** Every node captures its key ideas the moment it's
  shelved, drawn only from the source body and any human seed thoughts sent
  alongside it — never invented. Amended 2026-07-03: an earlier "no summaries,
  ever" rule assumed the recall loop ran; it didn't, so the ideas just
  evaporated. Distill first; let recall be the test of what's kept.
- **Append-only above, immutable below.** Human prose and marks are
  append-only — never erased or rewritten. A node's body is immutable once
  shelved; the bot owns only the distillation layers above it.
- **Sources are stable, concepts are plastic.** A source's distilled substance
  doesn't move once written. The connective concept layer is the bot's
  territory — relink, split, merge, demote freely — but concept substance is
  earned synthesis only: written when two or more sources genuinely converge,
  and cited to them.

### Boundaries
- **Containment.** Owns one vault folder; touches nothing outside it; isolated
  from other services it runs beside.
- **Least blast radius.** Unattended work is least-privilege and non-destructive;
  high-stakes or untrusted-web actions are gated behind a deliberate human step.
- **Nothing is lost.** Nothing is hard-deleted or dropped silently — processed
  items are archived, unprocessable ones logged with a reason.

### Behaviour
- **Quiet by default.** Silent when there's nothing worth surfacing.
- **Idempotent.** An already-processed item is never processed twice.
- **Session-budget aware.** Scope to the remaining Claude allowance; prefer work
  that finishes in-session; stop clean near limits rather than blow the plan.
